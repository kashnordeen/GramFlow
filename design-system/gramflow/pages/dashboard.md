# Dashboard Override: Obsidian Vault

The dashboard is an operational command center, not a marketing page.

## Hierarchy

1. Contextual greeting and attention summary.
2. Business Pulse inventory centerpiece.
3. Decision KPIs with truthful comparisons.
4. Sales/profit trend and stock health.
5. Action queue, recent activity, and quick actions.

## Dashboard-specific rules

- The layered inventory vault is the only rich 3D element.
- Lime represents primary action and stock health. Cyan is reserved for secondary analytical data.
- Charts use real server data, readable axes, text summaries, and non-color distinctions.
- When data is missing, show the action that creates it instead of a zero-filled decorative chart.
- Pointer depth is limited to 2-3 degrees and disabled for reduced motion or touch layouts.
- No balance is described as overdue because the current schema has no due date.
- The 7/30-day trend period is represented in the URL.
- Verify at 320px, 768px, 1024px, and 1440px.
